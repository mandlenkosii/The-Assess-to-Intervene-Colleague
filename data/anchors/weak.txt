Cellphones should not be used all the time in class. Sometimes they can help but sometimes they make problems. I think the teacher should say when we can use them because the teacher knows the lesson. If everybody uses a phone whenever they like, people will talk to friends and not listen.

A phone can help to find a word in a dictionary. This is useful if we are reading and do not understand a word. But not everybody has data or a good phone, so the teacher should give a book or paper too. It is not fair if one learner can finish because they have a phone and another learner cannot.

Phones can make a noise when a message comes. This can stop the class from listening. A learner might also take a photo of someone without asking. The teacher should make rules about phones and privacy. If phones are not needed, they should be in a bag.

Some learners say phones are important for emergencies. If something happens, the school office can help the learner contact their family. Learners can speak to the teacher if there is a real emergency. This means the class can still have a rule while people get help when they need it.

In conclusion, phones should only be used when the teacher says they are useful for a lesson. Everyone should get another option if they do not have a phone. This can help learners focus and make the rule fair. Every learner should have a chance to participate.
